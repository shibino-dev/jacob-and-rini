Our Story

Where It All Began

It all began with a little help from a matrimony website and a few very special people. Aravindettan, who has always been like an elder brother to us, along with Hari and a few close friends, helped bring two paths together. What started with two profiles and a simple introduction blossomed into something beautiful. Looking back, we see it not as coincidence, but as God's gentle plan, bringing two hearts together at the right time.

A Journey of Grace

As our conversations grew, so did a beautiful bond between us. Through shared dreams, prayers, and hopes, we discovered a future together. With every step, we felt God gently guiding our journey, leading us from a simple meeting to a relationship rooted in faith, understanding, and love.

A Promise of Forever

With hopeful hearts, we shared our journey with our families and sought their prayers and blessings. By God's grace, both families joyfully came together in February 2026, and 9 November 2026 was chosen as the day our forever begins.

The Day We Await

With grateful hearts, we step into a new chapter, trusting in God's plan that brought us together. We pray for His grace and guidance as we begin this beautiful journey of life together. 🤍
